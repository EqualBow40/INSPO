Why this style works

The wood-heavy palette makes the brand feel established and premium, while the soft cream surfaces keep the page light enough for anime and hobby products. It bridges “classy boutique” and “collector fun” instead of leaning too corporate or too childish.

  • Strong storefront identity from the first screen
  • Easy to swap illustrations with real product photos later
  • Works well for anime, Gunpla, collectibles, and card categories
  • Balances playful mascots with upscale product presentation

Brand Direction

You can treat this page as a visual starting point for a real storefront. The next step would be replacing the placeholder illustrations with actual category photos, your real logo asset, and live product data.
